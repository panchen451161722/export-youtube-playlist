## Email support

For product support, privacy requests, corrections, or feedback, email [support@exportyoutubeplaylist.com](mailto:support@exportyoutubeplaylist.com).

We aim to respond within three business days. Complex privacy, security, or third-party platform questions may take longer to investigate.

## Help us investigate

When reporting an export, connection, or collection problem, please include:

- The public source URL, if relevant, or the affected collection name without private content.
- The approximate time the problem occurred.
- The error message shown by the exporter.
- Your browser and device type.

Do not send passwords, Google credentials, API keys, payment details, private playlist links, or downloaded files containing information you consider confidential.

## Privacy and account requests

Public tools do not create a saved export history on their own. Playlist Expoter stores collection settings and metadata snapshots under the retention limits in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy). You can remove a collection, disconnect its YouTube account, or delete your website account from Settings → Profile. Disconnecting removes the selected connection and its associated private backups from active application storage; it cannot be undone. Original YouTube content is unchanged.

You can also revoke access at [Google Account permissions](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions) or email us to request access, correction, or deletion. Include the email address for your website account and the scope of your request, without tokens or private playlist contents. We may verify account ownership. Deletion of relevant YouTube data is completed as soon as possible and within seven calendar days after verification. See the Privacy Policy for provider recovery copies, technical logs, downloaded files, and already-delivered email.

For paid subscription changes, use Billing or the payment provider’s portal before deleting your website account. If you cannot access these controls, contact this support address; do not email payment-card details.

## Security reports

If you believe you found a security issue, describe the issue and the minimum steps needed to reproduce it. Please do not access other users' information, disrupt the service, or publicly disclose an unresolved issue.

## Copyright complaints

For a copyright complaint or DMCA notice, email [support@exportyoutubeplaylist.com](mailto:support@exportyoutubeplaylist.com) with the subject “Copyright / DMCA Notice.” Identify the copyrighted work, the exact page and material at issue, your contact information, the required good-faith and accuracy statements, and your physical or electronic signature. See the [Copyright Complaints and DMCA Notices section of our Terms of Service](/terms-of-service#copyright-complaints-and-dmca-notices) for the complete list.

## Non-affiliation

Playlist Expoter is an independent service and is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. Questions about YouTube accounts, videos, copyright decisions, or platform enforcement should be directed to YouTube.
