## A focused playlist export tool

Export YouTube Playlist is an independent web tool for turning public YouTube playlist metadata into clean CSV and Excel files. It is designed for people who want an organized list of videos without installing an extension, writing a script, or granting access to their YouTube account.

## Why it exists

YouTube playlists are excellent for watching, but they can be difficult to review as structured data. Students may want a course checklist, creators may want to compare publishing patterns, and music listeners may want a backup list of titles and links.

This tool makes that information easier to use in a spreadsheet while keeping the workflow small and understandable.

## How it works

When you submit a public playlist URL, our server requests available public metadata through the official YouTube Data API. The result is returned to your browser, where the selected CSV and XLSX files are generated for download.

The exporter does not ask for access to your YouTube account and does not create a saved history of the playlist URLs or exported metadata.

## What we value

- A useful free export should not require an account.
- Exported files should be clean, predictable, and spreadsheet-ready.
- Users should receive clear errors instead of broken downloads.
- Product limits and planned paid features should be stated plainly.
- Public metadata should be handled with care and only for the requested export.
