## Overview

Export YouTube Playlist helps you turn metadata from a public YouTube playlist into a CSV or XLSX file, or copy its video links. This policy describes the information involved when you use the service and the choices available to you.

The exporter does not require a user account. Optional Google sign-in is available for account features and future paid entitlements, but signing in does not grant this service access to your YouTube account. The service is designed not to create an application-level history of the playlist URLs you submit or the playlist data returned for your export.

## Information Processed by the Exporter

When you submit a playlist URL:

1. The URL is sent to our server so we can validate it and extract the playlist identifier.
2. Our server requests publicly available playlist and video metadata from the YouTube Data API.
3. The result is returned to your browser for preview.
4. CSV and XLSX files are generated in your browser. We do not upload the generated file back to our server.

The public metadata may include video titles, links, channel names, publication dates, durations, view counts, thumbnails, and playlist position. Private, unavailable, or deleted content may not be returned.

We do not use YouTube OAuth and do not ask for access to your YouTube account. Please do not submit private playlist links, credentials, API keys, or other confidential information.

## Storage and Technical Data

Submitted playlist URLs and returned playlist metadata are processed for the current request and are not intentionally stored in our application database. Your browser may temporarily keep the preview in memory until you reload or close the page.

Like most web services, our hosting and security providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, request status, and security events. Retention and handling by those providers are subject to their own policies and operational requirements.

## YouTube Data API

The service uses the YouTube Data API to retrieve public information. Google may receive the playlist identifier, our server's network information, and technical request details when we make an API request. Google's handling of that information is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

Export YouTube Playlist is an independent service and is not endorsed by or affiliated with YouTube or Google.
