## Overview

Playlist Expoter provides public metadata tools and an account-based Playlist Backup workspace. Public tools process the URL you submit for a preview or browser export. Creating a collection in Playlist Backup saves its source, settings, and metadata snapshots so you can review changes and export records.

We use YouTube API Services. YouTube and Google process information under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). This independent service is not endorsed by or affiliated with YouTube or Google.

## Public Tools and Browser Exports

Our server validates your public video, playlist, or channel URL, requests available metadata from the YouTube Data API, and returns the result to your browser. Metadata can include identifiers, titles, links, channel names, publication dates, durations, view counts, thumbnails, and playlist positions.

Public tools do not create a saved collection or application-level export history unless you separately use Playlist Backup. Download files are generated in your browser and are not uploaded back to our server. Previews may remain in browser memory until reload or closure. Hosting and security providers may still process technical request information.

We do not download or store YouTube video or audio files. Metadata exports cannot restore content removed from YouTube.

## Website Accounts and Separate YouTube Authorization

Website sign-in and YouTube connection are separate choices. Google sign-in supplies identity information for your website account and session, such as your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier. We also store account and session records and timestamps needed to operate and protect the account. We do not receive your Google password.

Choosing **Connect account** requests `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly`. This lets us read the connected YouTube channel's information and sources you select: private playlists you can access, liked videos, and subscribed channels. “Liked music” filters liked YouTube videos to the Music category; it is not the entire YouTube Music library and may differ from its Liked Music list. Watch Later is not supported.

We store the connected channel's identifier, title, thumbnail reference, connection status, authorization timestamps, and OAuth credentials needed for authorized requests. Dedicated YouTube connection credentials are encrypted before storage; this does not mean all account data or every identity-provider field is encrypted at the application level.

Offline authorization lets the server refresh access for features you enable. Scheduled checks run only when scheduling is activated and the collection is enabled with a valid entitlement. The permission does not allow editing playlists, likes, or subscriptions, or uploading or deleting videos.

## Saved Collections and Optional Notifications

Playlist Expoter stores the collection source and applicable URL, selected connection, name, check frequency, notification choices, check timestamps, recent snapshots, and metadata for items detected as missing. These records belong to your website account and are not published to other users. They support display, comparison, checks, and CSV, Excel, or JSON export. Each check currently supports up to 500 source items and at most five recent snapshots within the retention period below.

Historical snapshots are dated records, not claims about current YouTube content. Unavailable metadata cannot be backed up reliably.

Email alerts and JSON backup delivery require a configured delivery service, your enabled preferences, and a verified recipient email. When enabled, the selected provider—Resend or Cloudflare Email Service—processes the recipient address and notification content, which may include collection metadata, change details, and a JSON snapshot attachment. Manual checks do not send these scheduled notifications. Copies delivered to your mailbox are controlled by you and your email provider.

## Retention, Disconnecting, and Deleting Data

Snapshot and missing-item YouTube API metadata is retained for no more than 30 days after retrieval. New retrievals can create new current records but do not extend an old snapshot's lifetime. Pausing a collection or ending a subscription does not suspend this limit. Expired metadata is excluded from display and export and removed by cleanup. Collection settings you supplied may remain while the collection exists so you can resume checks later.

- **Remove collection:** deletes its saved metadata and associated queued notification data from the active application database and stops its checks. The source on YouTube is unchanged.
- **Disconnect a YouTube account:** after confirmation, stops access through that connection and removes its connected-account data, associated private collections, snapshots, missing-item history, and queued notification data from the active application database. We also request revocation from Google. Removal cannot be undone; reconnecting does not recover deleted snapshots.
- **Delete website account:** **Settings → Profile** removes your account, sessions, and associated collection data from the active application database and requests revocation for all its YouTube connections. Billing, security, or legally required records may be retained only where necessary for those separate purposes.
- **Revoke through Google:** remove access at [Google Account permissions](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Background authorization checks are scheduled daily, independently of collection frequency. When maintenance detects confirmed revocation, it removes the associated data. If authorization cannot be revalidated, access stops and associated data is removed no later than 30 days after the last successful validation.

Local removal does not wait for Google's response. If revocation fails temporarily, a separate queue may retain the minimum encrypted credential needed to retry for up to seven days. It is used only for revocation, not to read YouTube data, and removed after completion or expiry. Revocation can affect other channels or Google features sharing the grant; they may require reconnection.

You can also request deletion at [support@exportyoutubeplaylist.com](mailto:support@exportyoutubeplaylist.com). After verifying account ownership, we delete the relevant YouTube data as soon as possible and within seven calendar days. We never request your Google password or OAuth tokens.

These controls remove data from active application storage. They do not remotely delete your downloads, delivered email, or Google's original data. Hosting-provider recovery copies and technical logs have separate operational retention; we do not promise all such copies are erased immediately. Contact us if your request also concerns those records.

## Cookies, Analytics, Security, and Advertising

Essential cookies and local storage support sessions, authentication, language, theme, and security. Infrastructure providers such as Cloudflare may process IP addresses, browser information, timestamps, and security events. Turnstile, when enabled, evaluates technical signals to prevent automated abuse.

Optional analytics on logged-out public pages may include Microsoft Clarity, Cloudflare Web Analytics, or other configured providers. Clarity is configured without analytics or advertising cookies but can still process page paths, clicks, and scrolling. Third-party analytics, session recording, ads, and customer-service scripts are not loaded on account, authentication, collection, or backup pages, or on public pages served to signed-in users. Submitted URLs and public result areas are masked where public-page recording is used; we do not intentionally include source identifiers, titles, or account details in custom analytics events.

Advertising, including Google AdSense, operates only where configured and enabled. Vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers under applicable regional consent choices. Authorized Google user data is not used for ads. See [Google's partner-site information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/), and your browser controls. Where Google requires a certified consent-management platform, advertising must wait until that consent flow is available.
