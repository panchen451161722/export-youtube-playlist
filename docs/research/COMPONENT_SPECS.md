# Component specifications

## Header

- Sticky translucent header, 64-72px tall.
- Left: compact play-sheet product mark and `Export YouTube Playlist`.
- Center/right: anchor links; mobile sheet menu.
- Do not show sign-in in the first-release marketing header.

## Hero and Exporter

- Desktop two-column composition within 1200px.
- Copy: eyebrow, strong H1, concise subhead, three trust points.
- Exporter card: URL input, format badges, free-limit helper, primary preview button.
- Result panel replaces the lower card area without shifting the whole layout unexpectedly.
- Preview table shows at most 12 visible rows; exports include all returned rows.

## Proof strip

- Three compact statements with simple line icons.
- Use a bordered white surface crossing the hero/background boundary.

## Features and How it works

- Features: three differentiated cards; one larger card may span two columns on desktop.
- How it works: three numbered steps connected visually on desktop and stacked on mobile.

## Pricing

- Two cards only: Free and Pro.
- Free emphasizes 500 items and all launch export formats.
- Pro is visually aspirational but the action is disabled and labeled `Coming soon`.

## FAQ and footer

- Accessible single-open accordion.
- Footer contains only relevant product, legal, and non-affiliation text.

## Motion and accessibility

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Visible focus rings, semantic landmarks, explicit labels, 44px minimum tap targets.
- Do not rely on color alone for errors or status.
