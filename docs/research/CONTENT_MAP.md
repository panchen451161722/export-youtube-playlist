## SEO intent

Primary query: `export YouTube playlist to CSV`.

Secondary queries: `YouTube playlist to Excel`, `download YouTube playlist metadata`, `copy all YouTube playlist links`.

The homepage title, description, H1, FAQ, canonical, alternate-language links, sitemap, robots, and llms files must be coherent with this intent.
